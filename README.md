# ecommerce-sales-customer-analytics
# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

An end-to-end business intelligence project built with Power BI to analyze
e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability,
sales channels, and business growth opportunities.

---

##  Project Overview
<img width="1450" height="791" alt="dashboard1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0dc3a99f-3363-407a-a4ee-19bf11d91c07" />


The objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce data into
actionable business insights that can support decisions across:

- Sales performance
- Revenue and profitability
- Customer acquisition and retention
- Product performance
- Sales channel performance
- Customer lifetime value
- Business growth opportunities

The project follows a business-focused analytics approach:

Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Data Analysis → KPI Development → Dashboard → Business Insights

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis answers key business questions:

1. How is revenue and sales performance changing over time?
2. Which categories and products generate the most revenue?
3. Which categories contribute the most profit?
4. Which regions generate the highest revenue?
5. How strong is customer repeat purchasing?
6. How many new vs returning customers are there?
7. Which sales channels contribute the most revenue?
8. Which customers have the highest lifetime value?
9. Where are the biggest growth opportunities?
10. What actions can management take based on the analysis?

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Stable Revenue Base

Annual revenue remained broadly stable across the analyzed years,
indicating a relatively mature revenue base with limited year-over-year
expansion.

### 2. Strong Repeat Purchasing

22.9K customers placed three or more orders, indicating that repeat
purchasing is an important component of customer activity.

### 3. Customer Acquisition Opportunity

Only 334 customers were classified as new customers in the analyzed
customer period, highlighting an opportunity to strengthen customer
acquisition.

### 4. Strong Digital Sales Channels

The Mobile App and Website generated the majority of total revenue,
making these channels important for customer conversion and retention.

### 5. Product Profitability

Electronics generated the highest category profit at approximately
₹4.90M, making category-level profitability an important area for
product strategy.

### 6. Year-End Sales Peak

Monthly sales reached approximately ₹9.1M in December, indicating
strong year-end demand.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Strengthen new-customer acquisition campaigns.
- Use personalized offers to improve customer conversion.
- Increase AOV through product bundles and cross-selling.
- Focus on high-margin product categories.
- Monitor products with high sales but weak profitability.
- Optimize Mobile App and Website conversion.
- Develop retention campaigns for repeat customers.
- Investigate the causes of relatively flat annual revenue.
